The difference

Career exploration should create ownership, not just answers.

Traditional tools often begin by matching interests to occupations. Career Navideer starts with lifestyle intent, introduces real-world constraints, and lets learners work through the tradeoffs.

01Lifestyle

Define the life the learner wants to support.

02Income

Translate choices into a realistic salary target.

03Careers

Explore occupations capable of supporting that target.

04Education

See preparation, skills and pathway requirements.

05My Career Path

Turn exploration into a revisitable personal route.

Why it matters

“What do you want to be?” is often the wrong opening question.

When learners cannot yet name an occupation, career conversations stall. Lifestyle-first planning gives them something concrete to react to: how they want to live, what that life costs, and what choices make it possible.
