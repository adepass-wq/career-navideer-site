Career Navideer® for Higher Education

Help students connect education to the lives and careers they are building.

Career Navideer® gives colleges and universities a structured way to connect lifestyle goals, occupational evidence, program choices and career preparation. Students can explore broadly, make decisions visible and build a Career Path that advisors and career professionals can use in focused conversations.

3 Examine the evidence

4 Assess career readiness

In My Career Path, the student reviews occupation-specific knowledge, abilities and skills and identifies development gaps.

5 Build and review the plan

The student sets goals and timelines for experience, networking, mentoring and skill development, then reviews the saved plan with an advisor.

What students can examine

Career decisions require more than a list of suggested occupations.

Career Navideer® gives students enough information to compare options, understand preparation requirements and decide what they need to do next.

CAREER SEARCH

50,000 job titles and 1,100 occupation profiles

Students can begin with a job title they already know or explore occupations aligned with their lifestyle, interests, values and education preferences.

LABOR MARKET INFORMATION

Earnings and workforce demand

National and regional earnings, employment outlook and projected demand help students evaluate opportunity alongside personal fit.

THE WORK ITSELF

Tasks, context, values and activities

Students can examine what people do, how the work is organized and which work values and activities matter within an occupation.

PREPARATION

Education, credentials and programs

Occupation reports connect career goals to education levels, certifications and accredited programs rather than leaving students to search for preparation on their own.

CAREER READINESS

Knowledge, abilities and skills

My Career Path helps students assess occupation-specific requirements, identify gaps and plan development through coursework, research, work experience and other activities.

CONTINUING DEVELOPMENT

Mentoring, networks and self-care

Students can set goals and timelines for professional networks, mentors, self-care and time management, then return to revise the plan.
