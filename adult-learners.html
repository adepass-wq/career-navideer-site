Career Navideer® for Adult Learners

Every level of education. One platform for building the path.

Career Navideer® serves adults who did not complete high school, people in job-training and workforce programs, college students, working professionals, graduate students and learners pursuing professional degrees or doctorates. It connects the life they want with occupations, education requirements and the capabilities they need to develop.

What Career Navideer® does

Connect lifestyle, occupations, education and action.

The platform brings the parts of a career decision together so adults can explore broadly, investigate carefully and move forward with a plan they understand.

LIFESTYLE AND INCOME

Begin with the life you want

Use choices about location, housing, transportation, family life, eating and luxuries to make lifestyle priorities and salary requirements visible.

OCCUPATIONAL EVIDENCE

Investigate real career options

Search more than 50,000 job titles mapped to over 1,100 occupation profiles. Examine earnings, regional and national demand, work context, education, interests, values, tasks and related occupations.

EDUCATION NAVIGATION

Find the preparation route

Connect an occupation to required education, credentials, certifications and accredited programs instead of stopping at a list of suggested careers.

What adults can compare

See the work, the preparation and the tradeoffs before choosing.

Adults often make career decisions while managing work, family, finances and limited time. Career Navideer® brings the information needed for those decisions into one connected experience.

LIFESTYLE FIT

Location, income and daily life

Connect housing, transportation, family life and other priorities to the income a desired lifestyle may require.

OCCUPATION FIT

Duties and work conditions

Review tasks, work context, work activities, interests and values to understand what the occupation actually involves.

OPPORTUNITY

Earnings and demand

Compare national and regional earnings, employment outlook and projected demand across occupations.

PREPARATION

Education and credentials

See whether an occupation requires employer training, an apprenticeship, a certificate, a degree, professional education or doctoral preparation.

CURRENT CAPABILITY

Knowledge, abilities and skills

Assess existing strengths against occupation-specific requirements and identify what needs further development.

NEXT ACTION

Goals and timelines

Use My Career Path to plan training, education, experience, networking and mentoring, then return to update the plan.

My Career Path

Turn exploration into career readiness.

After selecting an occupation or job title, adults can examine the knowledge, abilities and skills associated with that work, assess where they are now and build short-term and long-term development plans. My Career Path also supports planning for networking, mentoring, self-care and time management.

Career Navideer® in Higher Education

Give adult, graduate and professional learners a clearer connection between education and work.

Colleges and universities can use Career Navideer® as part of advising, career services, adult degree completion, graduate education, first-year experience and workforce-aligned programs. Students retain ownership of the decision while advisors gain a concrete basis for more focused conversations.

ADULT DEGREE COMPLETION

Make the value of additional education visible

Help returning learners weigh prior education, work experience, family responsibilities, financial requirements and the preparation needed for a career goal.

GRADUATE AND PROFESSIONAL EDUCATION

Translate advanced training into career possibilities

Connect disciplinary expertise to occupations, workforce demand, required capabilities and the steps needed for academic, research, industry and other professional careers.

ADVISING AND CAREER SERVICES

Move from general advice to specific evidence

Use saved occupations, education routes, capability assessments, development gaps, goals and timelines to guide timely conversations and referrals.

For institutions: Career Navideer® supports learner, counselor and administrative roles. Institutional implementations can be organized by campus, program, course, cohort or grant-funded initiative and can provide participation and pathway information for evaluation when interpreted with appropriate limits.

The adult learner opportunity

Stopping out does not have to mean staying out.

Adults need a practical way to connect prior learning, current responsibilities, career goals and a realistic route to completion. Career Navideer® helps make those choices visible before a learner commits more time or money.

This is a descriptive finding from the current research population. It does not establish causation or represent every Career Navideer® user.

A related option for career transitions

When the central challenge is changing careers.

Transition Navideer is a sister product designed for adults making a defined career transition. It helps people recognize transferable knowledge, abilities and skills, compare them with the requirements of a new occupation and plan how to close specific gaps. Career Navideer® remains the broader platform for lifestyle-centered career exploration, education navigation and continuing career development.
