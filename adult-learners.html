Adult Learners

Career planning should meet adults where they actually are.

Career Navideer supports adults across an unusually broad range—from people who never completed high school to career changers, tradespeople, college graduates, professionals and learners considering advanced or postdoctoral pathways.

01No high-school credential

Clarify goals, identify realistic preparation routes and connect career ambitions to education options.

02GED / workforce re-entry

03Trades & technical pathways

Compare skill-based routes, training requirements and occupations that fit the life the learner wants.

04College & career change

Reassess whether current education and work choices still align with personal goals.

05Professional & advanced study

Explore specialized occupations and the education required for graduate, professional and postdoctoral aspirations.
