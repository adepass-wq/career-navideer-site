Solutions

The same question lands differently at every stage.

How do you want to live? Career Navideer adapts that question to learners making their first career decisions, students choosing pathways, and adults reconsidering what comes next.

K–12

Middle & High Schools

Use lifestyle choice to create a practical reason to care about careers, income and educational preparation.

Best fit: career readiness, advisory, CTE exposure, counseling and STEM programming.

HIGHER ED

Colleges & Universities

Help students connect major and career exploration to personal goals before indecision becomes disengagement.

Best fit: first-year experience, student success, career services and bridge programs.

PROGRAMS

STEM & Funded Initiatives

Add a structured career-development experience that can generate process data alongside program outcomes.

Best fit: broadening participation, workforce development, summer and research programs.

FAMILIES

Parents & Families

Replace dead-end conversations about job titles with a practical discussion about future life choices.

Best fit: guided conversations, financial literacy and shared planning.

ADULT

Career Transition

Start with the life someone wants next, then connect existing skills and new pathways to realistic options.

Best fit: adult learners, workforce organizations and career changers.

INSTITUTIONS

Institutional Implementation

Deploy licenses at the program or institutional level and align use with advising, curriculum or evaluation goals.

Best fit: organizations that want structured adoption rather than isolated student use.

Implementation

Start with the decision you want learners to be better prepared to make.

The strongest implementations are not “give students a login.” They place Career Navideer at a meaningful decision point and decide in advance what educators will do with the resulting conversation and data.

A practical implementation sequence

1. Define the learner decision point.

2. Choose the Career Navideer workflow.

3. Decide what educators will discuss or reinforce.

4. Select any external measures needed for evaluation.

5. Review patterns and refine the intervention.
