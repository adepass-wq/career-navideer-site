A traceable learner journey

Five connected moves—not a one-time career quiz.

These are the product functions observed in the Career Navideer learner experience. A prospective partner should be able to see each one in a live review.

  1. 1. Define the lifestyle.Learners use the Lifestyle Index to make choices about the life they want—the starting point for a practical conversation.
  2. 2. Surface the income requirement.The platform turns lifestyle choices into an annual income target, making the financial implications of a desired life visible.
  3. 3. Investigate occupations in depth.Learners explore tasks, earnings and employment trends alongside skills, abilities, knowledge, interests, work styles, values, job zones, certifications and related occupations.
  4. 4. Connect exploration to preparation.Career exploration leads into course suggestions, skills development and postsecondary options across university, community-college, trade and technical routes.
  5. 5. Keep a route over time.My Career Path tracks progress across occupation knowledge, skills, ability, knowledge acquisition, networks, self-care and mentors.

Evidence boundary

Developmental evidence is not a prediction.

Career Navideer can document what a learner explored, revised and selected. That record can support reflection and advising. It is not a diagnosis, a fit score, a prediction of success or proof that the platform caused an outcome.

What makes the difference

Most career exploration ends with a list. Career Navideer is built to retain the reasoning: the lifestyle a learner selected, the financial target it creates, the careers compared, the preparation considered and the next actions still open.
