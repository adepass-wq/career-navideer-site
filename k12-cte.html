Grades 6-12 & CTE

Career development that belongs inside the school day.

Young people need better questions before they need definitive answers. Career Navideer gives middle and high school learners an age-appropriate way to connect lifestyle, careers, course choices, postsecondary preparation and real next steps.

Two versions, different developmental needs

Middle school and high school are not the same product experience.

CTE relevance

Built to support modern career-readiness work.

The modernized National Career Clusters Framework organizes CTE around 14 Career Clusters, 72 Sub-Clusters and 12 Career Ready Practices. Career Navideer can help schools place career exploration and decision-making into that broader career-readiness context.

Alignment can be tailored to state and local standards rather than forcing every school into one generic national template.

How career thinking changes

Discovery first. Evaluation when learners are ready.

Our internal usage analysis shows that age matters. The same platform supports different decision work at different developmental stages.
