The origin of Career Navideer

A better question changed the conversation.

When “What do you want to be?” kept producing “I don’t know,” Paris Gamble tried something different with his son: “How do you want to live?” Career Navideer grew from what happened next.

The idea

From a family conversation to a platform.

Lifestyle gave the career conversation something concrete. Once the desired future became visible, income, occupation, education and preparation could all become part of the same discussion.

That insight became Career Navideer and, more broadly, Lifestyle Learning’s commitment to helping learners take an active role in academic and career decision-making.

Leadership

Built by people who understand learners, workforce pathways and complex systems.

Career Navideer brings together deep experience in workforce-development program design and evaluation with the technology expertise required to turn complex information into a usable decision experience.

CO-FOUNDER • CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER

Anthony L. DePass, Ph.D.

Anthony DePass is a national leader in STEM workforce development and a longtime educator, program designer and evaluator. For more than 25 years, he has developed, led, advised and evaluated initiatives serving learners from middle school through postdoctoral education.

His work focuses on how people discover opportunity, build preparation and move through education and workforce pathways. That experience helps Career Navideer connect an engaging learner experience with the practical needs of schools, colleges, workforce programs and other institutions.

CO-FOUNDER • CHIEF OPERATING AND PRODUCT OFFICER

Paris Gamble

Paris Gamble is a technology implementation and project-management leader with experience delivering complex Human Capital Management and Enterprise Resource Planning systems for organizations across the United States.

His ability to translate complicated systems into clear, usable experiences shaped Career Navideer’s lifestyle-first approach and its emphasis on helping people move from information to meaningful decisions and action.
