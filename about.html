About Career Navideer

It started when “I don’t know” was not a useful answer.

Career Navideer grew from a parent trying to have a better conversation about the future. The breakthrough was simple: stop asking for a job title and ask what kind of life the student wanted.

The origin

From a family conversation to a platform.

Paris Gamble’s early conversations with his son exposed a familiar problem: students are often expected to choose a career before they have enough context to make the question meaningful. Asking about lifestyle opened a different conversation—one where money, education, work and aspiration could be connected without starting from a fixed occupation.

That idea became Career Navideer and, more broadly, Lifestyle Learning’s commitment to helping learners take an active role in academic and career decision-making.

“How do you want to live?”

A better starting point can change the entire career conversation.
