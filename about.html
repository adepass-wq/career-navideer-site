The origin of Career Navideer®

A better question changed the conversation.

When “What do you want to be?” kept producing “I don’t know,” Paris Gamble tried something different with his son: “How do you want to live?” Career Navideer® grew from what happened next.

The idea

From a family conversation to a platform.

Lifestyle gave the career conversation something concrete. Once the desired future became visible, income, occupation, education and preparation could all become part of the same discussion.

That insight became Career Navideer® and, more broadly, Lifestyle Learning’s commitment to helping learners take an active role in academic and career decision-making.
