Inside the platform

Not a quiz. A workspace for making the future concrete.

Career Navideer gives learners a place to build a lifestyle, calculate what it requires, investigate occupations, connect preparation, and keep a personal route over time.

The learner workbench

One connected decision environment.

The value is not any single screen. It is the relationship between choices that are usually separated: desired lifestyle, income, careers, education and next steps.

01
⌂

Lifestyle Index

Learners express the life they are working toward through decisions about location, housing, eating, transportation, family and luxuries. Those selections create a concrete economic target rather than a vague aspiration.

02
$

Income target

The platform translates lifestyle choices into an annual income requirement, making the financial side of career choice visible early enough to discuss and reconsider.

03
✦

Occupation intelligence

Learners can examine work tasks, earnings and employment trends alongside knowledge, skills, abilities, interests, work styles, values, job zones, certifications and related occupations.

04
▣

Education navigation

Career exploration connects to preparation: middle- and high-school course suggestions, skills development, and postsecondary options across universities, community colleges, trade schools and technical programs.

05
↗

My Career Path

Selected occupations can become an ongoing route—not a one-time result—with progress across occupation knowledge, skills, ability, knowledge acquisition, networks, self-care and mentors.

06
◎

Personalized engagement

Avatar customization, saved career bookmarks, optional Guru guidance, English/Spanish support and an accessible dashboard give learners reasons to return and continue their exploration.

A developmental record, not a label

Progress should be visible without pretending it is destiny.

Career Navideer can retain a learner’s sessions, selected occupations, evolving lifestyle target and Career Path progress. That creates useful developmental evidence: what they explored, what they revised, and where support is needed next.
