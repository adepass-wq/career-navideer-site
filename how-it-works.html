How it works

A developmental journey, not a personality result.

Career Navideer lets learners make choices, encounter constraints, explore possibilities and revise their thinking. The value is in the process.

STEP 1

Build a lifestyle

Learners start with concrete choices about the adult life they imagine—housing, transportation, experiences and other costs.

STEP 2

See the income implication

Those choices produce a salary target, making lifestyle expectations financially visible.

STEP 3

Explore occupations

Learners examine careers in relation to income, duties, education, workplace values and labor-market information.

STEP 4

Interrogate the pathway

Education and skill requirements become part of the career conversation rather than an afterthought.

STEP 5

Build a route

Selected possibilities move into a personal Career Path learners can revisit as their thinking develops.

STEP 6

Learn from the process

Programs can study patterns in exploration and decision-making rather than relying only on a final career choice.

The design principle

Motivation becomes more concrete when the future feels personal.

Career Navideer was built around a simple observation: a learner who cannot answer “What do you want to be?” may still have a rich answer to “How do you want to live?”
