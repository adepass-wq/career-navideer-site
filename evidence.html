Evidence & Career Decision Intelligence

Study the process, not just the final choice.

Career Navideer creates a trail of aspirations, exploration and decisions. Career Decision Intelligence (CDI) is the framework for making that developmental process more visible and more useful.

Observed behaviorLinked validated measuresLongitudinal outcomes
What CDI is—and is not. CDI is a developmental evidence framework. It is not presented as a validated score, diagnosis, prediction system or proof that Career Navideer causes a particular outcome. That distinction is deliberate.

What the platform observes directly

Behavior first. Interpretation second.

Career Navideer directly captures lifestyle selections, career exploration, selected pathways and sequential activity. Constructs such as self-efficacy or future orientation should be linked from validated measures rather than inferred from clicks alone.
